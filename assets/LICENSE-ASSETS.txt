Where each file comes from (all from free CC0 downloads, nothing from a paid pack)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All files were taken unchanged from the free download
  https://vuena.itch.io/vuena-free-mega-starter  (68 CC0 assets, its LICENSE.txt is CC0 1.0)

characters/  8 Free Blocky Characters v1.3 (hero, knight, wizard, pirate, astronaut, robot, zombie, princess)
             from the free sample of "48 Rigged Characters"
pets/        Cute Pets & Wildlife FREE sample: cat, fox, owl (12 clips each, mobile LOD included)
props/       Blocky Winter & Christmas free sampler: snowy pine, decorated tree, lantern post, snowman,
             igloo, sled, candy cane, red and blue present

(Godot imports the .glb files and extracts their embedded textures as the .png files you see next to them.)

How they were made
------------------
The models, rigs, animations and textures were generated by my own procedural Blender Python code,
which I wrote with AI help. The meshes and textures themselves are not AI-generated.

The full, much bigger packs these samples were cut from are separate paid products with their own
licence and are NOT included here:
  https://vuena.itch.io/blocky-pets-wildlife
  https://vuena.itch.io/blocky-characters-48
  https://vuena.itch.io/blocky-character-creator
